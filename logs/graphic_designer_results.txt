7. Analytics Integration:
Add an analytics component that tracks users' behavior, preferences, and feedback for improving future app updates and enhancements.

8. Responsive Design:
Ensure the mobile application design is responsive across various devices and screen sizes while maintaining its overall functionality and visual appeal.
